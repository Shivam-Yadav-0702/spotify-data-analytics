# 🎵 Spotify Data Analytics — Capstone Project

## 📌 Objective
Analyze 170,000+ Spotify tracks to uncover music trends,
audio feature patterns and factors driving song popularity.

## 📊 Analysis Areas
- Popularity distribution across 170K+ tracks
- Songs released per year (digital music growth trends)
- Correlation between audio features (energy, danceability, loudness)
- Average popularity trends over time
- Explicit vs non-explicit content analysis
- Danceability vs Popularity relationship
- Top 10 most popular tracks identification

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Most songs have low-medium popularity; only a few achieve viral status
- Songs released post-2000 increased rapidly due to digital streaming growth
- Energy and loudness are strongly positively correlated
- Acousticness negatively correlates with energy
- Songs with moderate-high danceability tend to score better popularity
- Newer tracks score higher popularity due to active streaming attention
